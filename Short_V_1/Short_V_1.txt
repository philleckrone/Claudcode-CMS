SHORT VERSE 1
=============

PURPOSE
Flags chapters where verse 1 contains fewer characters than a minimum
threshold. A very short verse 1 often indicates that introductory content
is missing or that the text has been split incorrectly.

The check counts characters in the text of verse 1 up to the next paragraph
marker (\p). USFM markers and inline footnotes or cross-references are
excluded from the count.

HOW TO USE
1. Install the Phils Custom (cms) Checks plugin with Install_Phils_tools.exe. Everything comes with the plugin: nothing has to be copied into the cms folder.
2. In Paratext, choose Tools > Phils Custom (cms) Checks..., select this check and click "Run...". In Paratext's check window, choose the books and set the options (Options...) before you click OK.
3. Each result shows the reference, the actual character count, and the
   minimum required so you can quickly see how short the verse is.

OPTIONS
  MinChars
    Default:  40
    The minimum number of characters that verse 1 must contain. Chapters
    where verse 1 has fewer than this many characters are flagged.

  CheckOtherMarkers
    Default:  No
    When Yes, the check also looks for short text after the markers listed
    in OtherMarkers (e.g., poetry markers like \q1).

  OtherMarkers
    Default:  q q1 q2 q3 q4
    Space-separated list of paragraph markers to check in addition to \p
    and \m. Only used when CheckOtherMarkers is set to Yes.
